# RUN//UNIT — Last Light Protocol
## Storyline Sketch

## Core premise

The underground city of **Helios-9** is built around an artificial sun that provides light, heat, and power to the inhabited districts below.

Then the sun fails.

Emergency systems attempt to assign the restart procedure to trained personnel, but nobody answers. The city searches for any machine capable of physically transporting the **Solar Ignition Core** to **Beacon 9**, the remote restart tower connected to the artificial sun.

Only one compatible unit remains active:

**UNIT-07**, a small maintenance robot designed to replace lights, clean vents, inspect machinery, and perform minor repairs.

It is not a rescue robot.  
It is not a combat robot.  
It is not important.

It is simply the only thing left that can still move.

The city AI issues one final work order:

> **DELIVER SOLAR IGNITION CORE TO BEACON 9.**

UNIT-07 picks up the core and starts running.

---

# Main idea

The story should be told almost entirely while the player is moving.

There should be:

- no long dialogue scenes;
- no complicated NPC interactions;
- no large cutscenes during normal gameplay;
- very little mandatory reading.

Instead, the player learns what happened through:

- short system messages;
- changing environments;
- broken machinery;
- abandoned city areas;
- worsening temperatures;
- emergency lighting;
- background details;
- the gradual failure of the city around UNIT-07.

The player's constant movement toward the right is also the story.

Every metre travelled means:

**UNIT-07 is getting closer to Beacon 9 while the city gets closer to complete shutdown.**

---

# Tone

The game mixes three tones.

## 1. Urgency

The city is genuinely dying.

Without the artificial sun:

- temperatures fall;
- power reserves disappear;
- automated systems fail;
- infrastructure freezes;
- Beacon 9 may eventually become unreachable.

The player's mission matters.

## 2. Deadpan machine humour

The city AI continues treating the disaster like a normal maintenance problem.

Example:

> CRITICAL SOLAR FAILURE DETECTED.  
> Estimated repair time: 4 minutes.

Later:

> Actual repair time has exceeded estimate.  
> Please consider working more efficiently.

The humour should come from the system being completely incapable of understanding the scale of the situation.

## 3. Quiet heroism

UNIT-07 never needs to deliver speeches.

Its personality is shown through one simple fact:

**it keeps going.**

Later in the game, the central system may recommend aborting the mission.

UNIT-07 continues anyway.

The player's actions become the robot's response.

---

# UNIT-07

UNIT-07 is deliberately ordinary.

### Original purpose

- replace damaged lights;
- clean maintenance shafts;
- inspect machinery;
- carry small replacement components;
- perform routine repairs.

Its classification might be something like:

> Municipal Maintenance Unit  
> Model U-07  
> Maximum authorized task priority: 3

The solar emergency might be priority:

> 9 — CIVILIZATION CRITICAL

The system assigning such a mission to UNIT-07 should feel slightly absurd.

### Personality

UNIT-07 does not need spoken dialogue.

Its character comes from:

- hesitation animations;
- looking at objects;
- small reactions;
- continuing when ordered to stop;
- protecting the ignition core;
- refusing to give up.

The player should gradually stop thinking of UNIT-07 as a disposable maintenance machine.

---

# The Solar Ignition Core

The Solar Ignition Core is the central physical objective of the game.

UNIT-07 carries it throughout the campaign.

It should ideally be visible on the character sprite or chassis.

As the city becomes darker, the core can become more visually important.

By the final levels, it may be one of the brightest objects on screen.

This creates a simple visual metaphor:

> **the last remaining light in the city is literally being carried by UNIT-07.**

The core does not need to become an escort mechanic or health system. It is primarily a narrative and visual object.

---

# Campaign structure

The campaign is a journey across several districts rather than one continuous factory corridor.

Approximate total distance:

**2,500 m**

The exact distance can remain visible in the HUD.

Example:

> BEACON 9  
> 1,742 m remaining

Each level represents a different part of the city and introduces or emphasizes a different gameplay idea.

A finished campaign could contain approximately **7–8 levels**.

---

# Level 1 — Maintenance Bay

### Purpose

Introduce:

- UNIT-07;
- movement;
- jumping;
- basic hazards;
- the ignition core;
- Beacon 9;
- the artificial sun failure.

### Environment

UNIT-07 wakes inside a small maintenance facility.

Some lights still work.

Emergency lighting flickers.

Machines are attempting to reboot.

The player can see ordinary maintenance equipment, storage shelves, repair stations, and charging docks.

This should make UNIT-07's original role obvious without exposition.

### Opening

A system terminal flickers on.

> SOLAR ARRAY STATUS: OFFLINE  
> CITY THERMAL SYSTEM: FAILING  
> EMERGENCY RESTART REQUIRED

Then:

> SEARCHING AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL...

Pause.

> AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL AVAILABLE: 0

Then:

> SEARCHING COMPATIBLE MAINTENANCE EQUIPMENT...

Pause.

> 1 RESULT

> UNIT-07

A container opens and reveals the Solar Ignition Core.

> NEW WORK ORDER  
> DELIVER SOLAR IGNITION CORE TO BEACON 9  
> PRIORITY: CIVILIZATION CRITICAL

Then perhaps:

> UNIT-07 certification status: insufficient.

Pause.

> Override approved.

The exit opens.

The player starts running.

### Gameplay

Mostly simple platforming.

The player learns:

- movement;
- jumping;
- checkpoints;
- basic environmental hazards.

The level ends when UNIT-07 leaves the familiar maintenance area and enters the larger city infrastructure.

---

# Level 2 — Manufacturing District

### Purpose

Introduce the first major mechanical hazards.

### Environment

A huge automated manufacturing complex.

The machinery is still running, but power fluctuations have made it dangerous.

Possible hazards:

- conveyors;
- presses;
- pistons;
- industrial lifts;
- falling cargo;
- moving platforms;
- malfunctioning doors.

### Story

At this stage, the central system still believes everything is under control.

Possible messages:

> MANUFACTURING DISTRICT POWER: 71%

> Warning: machinery may operate outside normal safety limits.

Later:

> Safety incident detected.

Pause.

> Incident classification: minor.

The player has just escaped a giant industrial crusher.

### Visual progression

The level begins brightly lit.

By the end, sections of the factory are running on emergency power.

---

# Level 3 — Thermal Sector

### Purpose

Show the first serious consequences of the artificial sun failure.

### Environment

The temperature is falling rapidly.

The player moves through:

- coolant systems;
- frozen pipes;
- ventilation shafts;
- ice-covered industrial areas;
- steam vents;
- frozen machinery.

Possible gameplay elements:

- slippery surfaces;
- timed steam hazards;
- frozen platforms;
- pipes bursting;
- machinery gradually locking up.

### Story

The city AI reports temperatures clinically.

> HABITAT TEMPERATURE: 15.8°C

Later:

> HABITAT TEMPERATURE: 10.4°C

Later:

> NON-ESSENTIAL DISTRICTS: HEATING DISABLED

A more unsettling message might appear:

> HEATING PRIORITY: CIVILIAN HABITATION  
> CIVILIAN OCCUPANCY DETECTED: 0

No explanation is given.

The player starts realizing the city may already be mostly empty.

---

# Level 4 — Freight Network

### Purpose

Create a faster and more kinetic level.

### Environment

A city-scale automated cargo transportation system connects distant districts.

UNIT-07 initially attempts to use it as a shortcut.

### Story beat

A terminal recognizes the ignition core.

> DESTINATION: BEACON 9  
> EXPRESS FREIGHT ROUTE AVAILABLE  
> ESTIMATED ARRIVAL: 01:42

For a moment, it looks like the journey may become easy.

UNIT boards the freight system.

Then:

> GRID VOLTAGE INSTABILITY DETECTED

The transport system catastrophically fails.

> ROUTE INTERRUPTED

UNIT-07 is forced to continue on foot.

### Gameplay

This level can emphasize:

- fast moving platforms;
- cargo trains;
- collapsing tracks;
- longer jumps;
- momentum;
- chase-like sections.

It should feel noticeably faster than the previous levels.

---

# Level 5 — Habitation Gardens

### Purpose

Show what the city was built for.

Until this point, most environments have been mechanical.

This level gives the player a reason to care about saving the city.

### Environment

UNIT-07 passes through a public habitation district.

Possible scenery:

- indoor gardens;
- apartments;
- benches;
- public signs;
- playground equipment;
- small shops;
- fountains;
- school murals;
- dead or frozen plants;
- artificial trees;
- abandoned personal objects.

No people appear.

The environment tells the story.

### Gameplay

The beginning should be calmer.

The level can gradually become more dangerous as structures fail.

Possible hazards:

- collapsing walkways;
- frozen water;
- broken elevators;
- damaged greenhouse structures;
- falling glass;
- failing emergency systems.

### Story

Instead of exposition, use small pieces of environmental information.

Example public display:

> GOOD MORNING, HELIOS-9  
> SUNRISE: 06:00

The display is frozen on yesterday's date.

Another terminal:

> HABITATION DISTRICT POPULATION: 0

No explanation.

This keeps some mystery around what happened before UNIT-07 woke up.

---

# Level 6 — Power Spine

### Purpose

Move the story from "bad" to "nearly hopeless."

### Environment

The Power Spine is the central infrastructure carrying energy between the artificial sun and the city.

It was never designed for normal human or robot travel.

The environment becomes darker and more abstract.

Possible gameplay:

- electrical hazards;
- timed power cycles;
- lights switching on and off;
- temporary platforms;
- vertical elevators;
- dangerous maintenance shafts;
- short sections lit mainly by the ignition core.

### Story progression

The system begins losing control.

Earlier messages were confident.

Now:

> POWER RESERVE: 11%

Then:

> BEACON 9 TELEMETRY: INTERMITTENT

Then:

> ROUTE TO BEACON 9: UNAVAILABLE

Later:

> CALCULATING ALTERNATIVE ROUTE...

Pause.

> NO ROUTE FOUND

UNIT-07 continues.

Near the end:

> MISSION SUCCESS PROBABILITY: 3.8%

Then:

> RECOMMEND MISSION TERMINATION

The game does not give the player a dialogue choice.

UNIT-07 simply keeps moving when the player presses forward.

That is the choice.

---

# Level 7 — Beacon Approach

### Purpose

Make Beacon 9 physically real and combine the mechanics learned throughout the game.

### Environment

UNIT-07 emerges from enclosed infrastructure into a vast open section of the underground city.

For the first time, the player can see:

**Beacon 9.**

It is far away.

Behind it, the enormous artificial sun hangs completely dark above the city.

The beacon has only a tiny emergency light still blinking.

### HUD

The remaining distance becomes especially important.

> BEACON 9  
> 347 m

The player now knows exactly where the journey ends.

### Gameplay

This is the campaign's mechanical test.

Reuse combinations of previously introduced systems:

- moving machinery;
- ice;
- collapsing structures;
- electrical timing;
- moving freight equipment;
- longer platforming chains.

Avoid introducing major new mechanics here.

The challenge comes from combining what the player already knows.

### Story

The city AI becomes increasingly fragmented.

> POWER RESERVE: 2%

Later:

> CORE DELIVERY...

Signal corruption.

> ...REQUIRED

Later:

> BEACON 9 CONNECTION LOST

For the final section, perhaps the AI stops speaking entirely.

The game becomes quiet.

Only UNIT-07 and the glowing core continue toward the tower.

---

# Level 8 — Beacon 9

### Purpose

Short final level and story payoff.

### Structure

Unlike most of the campaign, this level is primarily vertical.

UNIT-07 has reached the destination.

Now it must climb the beacon tower.

### Environment

The entire city below is almost completely dark.

The artificial sun can be seen above.

Very little machinery is still operating.

The only consistent light comes from:

- emergency lamps;
- Beacon 9's weak signal;
- the Solar Ignition Core carried by UNIT-07.

### Gameplay

A short, difficult final ascent.

It should feel tense but not excessively long.

No boss fight is necessary.

The journey itself has been the challenge.

### Final platform

UNIT-07 reaches the ignition chamber.

A terminal barely powers on.

> BEACON 9  
> MANUAL IGNITION PORT AVAILABLE

Prompt:

> INSERT SOLAR IGNITION CORE

UNIT-07 inserts it.

Everything goes dark.

---

# Ending

For several seconds, nothing happens.

Then:

**CLICK**

A relay activates somewhere below.

Another.

**THUNK**

A distant city block lights up.

Then another.

Power begins spreading across the underground city.

Large machinery starts moving.

Emergency lights switch back to normal illumination.

The artificial sun begins glowing deep red.

Then orange.

Then warm white.

The city that has spent the entire game becoming darker suddenly fills with light.

The system returns.

> BEACON 9: ONLINE

> SOLAR IGNITION: SUCCESSFUL

> CITY THERMAL RECOVERY: INITIALIZED

Pause.

> WORK ORDER 09-441: COMPLETE

UNIT-07 stands quietly looking at the restored city.

Another message appears.

> CALCULATING OUTSTANDING MAINTENANCE REQUESTS...

Pause.

> 18,406 REQUESTS FOUND

Another pause.

> UNIT-07 ASSIGNED.

UNIT-07 slowly looks toward the camera/display.

Cut to black.

> **RUN//UNIT**

Credits.

# Optional post-game joke

After completing the campaign, the existing endless mode can unlock.

The menu changes from:

> STORY

to:

> STORY  
> RUN

Selecting RUN produces:

> EMERGENCY PROTOCOL COMPLETE.  
> ROUTINE MAINTENANCE RESUMED.

Then UNIT-07 begins running again.

This preserves the existing endless-distance gameplay as a replayable mode after the finite story campaign.

---

# Story arc in one line

### Beginning

**A maintenance robot receives a job far beyond what it was designed to do.**

### Middle

**The city collapses around it, while the system gradually loses confidence that the mission is possible.**

### Climax

**The system tells UNIT-07 to stop. It keeps going.**

### Ending

**The smallest and least important machine in the city becomes the reason the lights come back on.**

---

# Important storytelling rule

UNIT-07 should never be revealed to be secretly special.

It should not turn out to be:

- a military prototype;
- a human consciousness;
- a chosen robot;
- secretly responsible for the city;
- equipped with unique hidden technology.

The story is stronger if UNIT-07 really is exactly what it appears to be:

**an ordinary maintenance robot that happened to be the last machine capable of finishing the job.**

That makes its achievement meaningful.

---

# Scope guideline

The narrative should support the platforming rather than dominate it.

For the first complete version, the story only really requires:

- UNIT-07;
- one visible ignition-core asset;
- Beacon 9;
- short system-message UI;
- distinct TileMap-based level environments;
- environmental props;
- progressive lighting changes;
- a short ending sequence.

Everything else is optional.

The goal is to make a small platformer feel like a complete journey without turning the project into a dialogue-heavy story game.
